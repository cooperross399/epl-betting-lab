How to read this

  • Best bets only, at the stake the card carried that week. Rows marked * were posted before the stake was recorded and count at {{ defaultUnits }}u.
  • Leans, voids and unpriced entries are not counted. The Due List is a watch list, never a bet, and is not here.
  • Small samples swing. A club at +3u after 11 bets is one bad weekend from zero. Faded rows are too few to read.
  • Measured against real prices, no market shows a demonstrated edge. A positive season here is a sample, not proof.
